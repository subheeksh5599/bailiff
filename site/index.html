We read the current payer and specialist requirements, then version them.

Requirements change without notice. Sentry reads the live payer and specialist pages at intake and records exactly which version the case was checked against, so any decision can be audited later.

→ See how a case is verified

Sentry sends the real follow-up and records every reply against the case.

Chasing a referral is email work. Sentry sends the next message to the payer or the specialist, reads what comes back, and attaches each reply to the case as evidence instead of leaving it in someone inbox.

A case closes on confirmed handoffs, not on sent messages, and never on a guess.

Every open case shows what is still missing and who owes it. A case can only reach complete when the receiving clinic confirmation has actually been read back and matches the requirements frozen at intake.
